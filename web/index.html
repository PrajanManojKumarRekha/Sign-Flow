How to use
  • Face the camera with good lighting and a plain background.
  • Hold each letter steady until the ring fills. Lower your hand to repeat the same letter.
  • The backspace gesture deletes a character. Punctuation and spaces use the buttons or keyboard for now.
  • J and Z involve motion and are less reliable in single-frame detection.
